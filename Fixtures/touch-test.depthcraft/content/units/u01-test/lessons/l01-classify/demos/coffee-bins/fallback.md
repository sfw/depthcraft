# Coffee Shop Classification

## Learning Goal
Practice divergent-convergent thinking by classifying coffee shop names into concept bins.

## The Pattern

Coffee shop names fall into several conceptual categories:

### Time/Daily Routine
- The Daily Bean
- Daily Grind
- Sunrise Sips
- Morning Ritual
- First Light Coffee
- First Steam
- Daybreak Roasters

### Coffee Puns
- Brew Ha Ha
- Brewtiful Mornings
- Perk Up
- Bean Counter
- Grounds for Thought
- Full of Beans
- Brewed Awakening

### European/Romantic
- Caffè Luna
- Dolce Vita Coffee
- Casa Caffè
- Amore Espresso

### Descriptive/Literal
- Bean & Bloom
- The Steaming Cup
- Steamhouse Coffee

### Community/Place
- Steam & Story
- The Brew Crew

### Coffee Process
- The Grind House

## The Exercise

When brainstorming names, you first diverge (generate many options) then converge (classify and evaluate). This classification reveals patterns and helps you identify which conceptual territories you've explored and which remain untapped.
